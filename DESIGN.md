## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, editorial-grade creative educational environment. It balances the precision of an international design atelier with the warmth and approachability required for active, transformational mentorship. The interface is engineered for digital creators mastering industry-standard software suites (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro, InDesign, and Procreate), providing a focused canvas that lets student portfolios and instructor demonstrations take center stage.

The design movement combines **Minimalist Editorial Structure** with **Chromatic Tool Accents**. The architecture is structured around clean negative space, razor-sharp typographic discipline, and soft tactile surfaces. Rather than competing with creative visual assets, the interface acts as an architectural frame: quiet, luminous, and refined, punctuated by precise semantic indicators that represent distinct creative mediums and verified mentor credentials.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, high-clarity light canvas with rigorous visual discipline.

### Base & Brand Colors
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAFC` / `#FFFFFF`):** High-key off-white backdrop that prevents optical fatigue during deep-dive tutorial sessions while preserving luminous contrast.
- **Primary Brand (`#4F46E5`):** A deep, electric indigo embodying focus, intellectual discipline, and executive polish. Reserved for top-tier actions, progress tracking, and key navigation states.
- **Verified Mentor Teal (`#0D9488`):** Authoritative, calm, and trustworthy. Applied to verified badges, credential verification stamps, and active consulting status indicators.
- **Creative Accent Coral (`#F97316`):** Vibrant warm orange used sparingly for active critiques, live broadcast pings, and milestone celebrations.
- **Ink Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Pure slate-charcoal used for text hierarchy to ensure maximum legibility without harsh absolute black.

### Software Identity Badges (Semantic Tokens)
To orient students across multidisciplinary creative pipelines, standardized badge colors are applied exclusively to metadata tags, category filters, and course modules:
- **Photoshop Track:** `#31A8FF` (Cyan Blue)
- **Illustrator Track:** `#FF9A00` (Warm Amber)
- **After Effects Track:** `#9999FF` (Soft Periwinkle Violet)
- **Premiere Pro Track:** `#EA77FF` (Vibrant Orchid)
- **InDesign Track:** `#FF3366` (Editorial Crimson)
- **Procreate Track:** `#7B61FF` (Prismatic Iris Purple)

These chromatic accents never overwhelm functional containers; they appear strictly within badges, pill borders, and 2px left-rail indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, architectural presence of **Epilogue** for display headings with the hyper-legible, geometric neutrality of **Manrope** for learning materials, critique feeds, and interface interactions.

### Multilingual Support (Thai and English)
To maintain structural consistency across Latin and Thai scripts:
- When rendering Thai typography, system fallback falls smoothly to modern loopless faces (such as *Prompt* or *IBM Plex Sans Thai*), matched proportionally in x-height to Manrope.
- Maintain an increased 1.6 to 1.7 line-height ratio on all Thai body blocks to guarantee diacritic clearance and prevent vowel clipping.
- Avoid all-caps transformations on mixed-language badge labels to preserve Thai glyph syntax integrity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point rhythmic grid with a max canvas constraint of 1440px to ensure maximum workspace density without stretching video player modules or student submission canvases beyond comfortable visual angles.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px), `margin` (2rem / 32px) exterior pad. Ideal for split-screen mentoring setups: side-by-side video playback with live project commentary.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), `margin-tablet` (1.5rem / 24px). Sidebar collapses into an off-canvas drawer or sticky bottom utility bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px), `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Single-column stack with persistent bottom sheet actions for scheduling and curriculum navigation.

Rhythm throughout containers remains strictly uncompressed. Component padding never drops below `space-md` on interactive touch targets to facilitate rapid stylus and touch usage.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Architectural Outlines** coupled with **Luminous Ambient Diffusion**.

### Elevation Scale
1. **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Clean surface `#FAFAFC` with no borders and zero elevation. Applied to main background canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine, low-contrast border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Underpinned by a soft ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Floating Controls):** Activated on card hover, secondary popovers, and sticky navigation bars. Uses `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Video Review Overlays):** Concentrated focus layers. Backdrops utilize `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with an 8px blur filter. Modal panels float with `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`.

All elevations feature crisp, tailored edge borders to maintain visual clarity regardless of screen calibration or display brightness.

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of **2**, elements present balanced curvature that avoids clinical severity while retaining crisp geometric cohesion.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline tooltips, dropdown triggers, and data table rows.
- **Containers & Master Cards (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Course overview modules, live stream stage containers, and mentor profile summary cards.
- **Hero & Modal Surfaces (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Portfolio gallery showcase cards, checkout panes, and consultation booking flyouts.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Pills:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for software tags, lesson timestamps, and mentor status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Indigo background (`#4F46E5`), crisp white text, 0.5rem border radius. Micro-transition on hover shifts to `#4338CA` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), text in `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive / Caution:** Soft coral surface with high-contrast text for discarding work-in-progress submissions or canceling consultation slots.

### Software Identity Chips & Badges
- Constructed with a two-tone pill pattern: a light 10% opacity tint of the respective tool color as the badge background, paired with high-contrast text and a 2px left dot or icon representing the application (e.g., Photoshop Blue `#31A8FF` background at 10% fill with `#0077CC` text).
- Always set at `label-md` or `label-sm` with a height of 28px, maintaining internal horizontal padding of 12px.

### Cards
- **Curriculum & Lesson Cards:** Composed on Level 1 elevation with a 16:9 thumbnail region at the top (featuring a 1rem radius inset). A subtle software badge is pinned to the top-right corner. The card body uses `space-md` internal padding, featuring title typography in `headline-sm`, instructor meta in `body-sm`, and an integrated progress bar along the bottom border.
- **Consulting Mentor Cards:** Features a circular avatar with a verified teal check badge (`#0D9488`) positioned at the bottom-right of the avatar. Outlines mentor specializations via multi-software badge clusters.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focused state shifts border to `#4F46E5` with an external focus ring of `3px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- Input labels use `label-lg` in `#0F172A`, with supportive helper text in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Segmented tab switches (e.g., switching between "Curriculum", "Project Files", and "Critique Feed") use an enclosed capsule style with an off-white background track (`#F1F5F9`) and a pure white pill active indicator elevated at Level 1.
